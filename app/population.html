Population

123,975,371 people — and falling every year.

Japan's population peaked at 128.1 million in 2008 and has fallen every year since. In 2024 it dropped by 541,000, the largest annual fall in the ten years shown here.

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators (SP.POP.TOTL), accessed 26 November 2026

An ageing country

Nearly three in ten people in Japan are 65 or older — the highest share of any large country. The share has risen every year shown: 28.6% in 2019, 29.8% in 2024.

Children are the mirror image: the 0–14 share fell from 12.4% in 2019 to 11.4% in 2024, as the fertility rate fell from 1.36 to 1.15 births per woman.

Life expectancy remains among the world's highest at 84.0 years in 2024.

Total fertility rate, births per woman. Source: World Bank WDI (SP.DYN.TFRT.IN).
