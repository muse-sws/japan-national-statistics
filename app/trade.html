Trade

Japan sells $921 billion abroad — and buys $958 billion.

Exports of goods and services reached $921 billion in 2024, equal to 22.0% of GDP — the highest share in the six years shown. Imports were $958 billion, leaving a goods-and-services deficit of about $37 billion.

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators (NE.EXP.GNFS.CD, NE.IMP.GNFS.CD, NE.EXP.GNFS.ZS), accessed 26 November 2026

The 2022 deficit

Japan ran a goods-and-services deficit in every year shown, but 2022 was exceptional: imports jumped to $1,077 billion as energy prices spiked and the yen fell, pushing the deficit to $159 billion — more than six times the 2021 figure. By 2024 the deficit had narrowed to $37 billion.

What Japan trades

Machinery, vehicles and electronics dominate exports; mineral fuels, machinery and food dominate imports. Japan imports almost all of its oil and gas, which is why energy prices move the trade balance more than any other single factor.

Exports equalled 22.0% of GDP in 2024 — up from 15.1% in the pandemic year 2020.
