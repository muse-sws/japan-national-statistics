Economy

A $4.19 trillion economy — smaller in dollars, not in yen.

Japan's nominal GDP in US dollars fell from $5.25 trillion in 2019 to $4.19 trillion in 2024. The fall is mostly the yen: in yen terms the economy kept growing. Japan remains the world's fourth-largest economy.

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD), accessed 26 November 2026

Reading the numbers

Nominal vs real. These are nominal figures in current dollars, so they move with both prices and the exchange rate. For growth in the volume of goods and services, use real GDP — the Cabinet Office publishes it in the national accounts.

The yen effect. Between 2021 and 2024 the yen fell from about ¥110 to about ¥150 per dollar. Measured in yen, Japan's economy grew over the same period; measured in dollars, it shrank by a fifth.

Rank. In 2024 Japan was the world's fourth-largest economy in nominal dollars, behind the United States, China and Germany.
